## New Features

- **Screen recording.**
  - Press **F12** while playing to start a recording, and press it again to stop. While recording, a small red **REC** indicator with a timer shows in the corner.
  - When the recording ends, NEXUS shows *Recording saved*. If a recording cannot be made, NEXUS says why.
  - Recordings are MP4 files in `Media\Recordings` (or a folder you choose). They appear in the Media tab with their game and date.
  - Only the game window is recorded, never the NEXUS screens. The game's own sound is recorded, and your microphone can be added as a separate track.
  - NEXUS checks the PC for a hardware video encoder (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD or Intel Quick Sync) and uses it when present. Otherwise it encodes in software and offers resolutions only up to 1080p, to keep games smooth.
  - *Settings › Screen recording* covers:
    - on/off
    - the shortcut (key combinations such as Ctrl+F9 are allowed)
    - resolution and frame rate
    - quality or a custom bit rate
    - H.264 or HEVC (HEVC only when your graphics card can encode it)
    - game sound and microphone
    - the save folder
  - Recordings are written as they are made. If the PC or NEXUS stops during a recording, the part already recorded is kept and appears as *(interrupted)* the next time NEXUS starts.
- **Screenshots move to Alt+F12**, so F12 is free for recording. If your screenshot key was F12, NEXUS changes it to Alt+F12 for you; any other key you chose is kept.
- **Games keep running on NEXUS Home.**
  - Pressing Home in a game shows NEXUS Home while the game carries on in the background, muted and ignoring your controls.
  - A green dot and a *running in the background* banner show this, with **Resume** and **Close game**.
  - One press of Home is always one trip to Home.
  - If you prefer the game to stop, choose *Settings › Game sessions & saves › When you press Home in a game › Pause the game*.
  - The record and screenshot shortcuts also work while you are on Home with a game in the background.
- **Import saves from Lichii.**
  - *Save Data › Import* has a **From Lichii** option. It reads Lichii's save folder on this PC and shows which player and game each save belongs to, including its slots, account and option data.
  - You choose the NEXUS user to import into.
  - Lichii's own files are only read, never changed. A save already in NEXUS is replaced only after you confirm, and only after a backup.
- **Publisher details.** In *Properties › Details*, `NEXUS.exe` now lists *Nexus Development* as the company and NEXUS as the product, with the version number. This is the program's own description, not a digital signature (see *Known Issues*).

## Improvements

- **Lower CPU use while NEXUS is open.** The interface no longer does work on every screen refresh when nothing changes. NEXUS's interface work while idle, measured on the same test PC and library:
  - Home: from 5.3 % to 0.3 % of a CPU core;
  - Library: from 12.3 % to 0.4 %.

  This leaves more of the PC for a game running in the background.
  - The focus highlight, the running-game dot and the loading bar now animate on the graphics card.
  - Controller polling slows down when no controller is connected.
- **Appearance:** the *Appearance* page now always shows the theme and accent colour currently in use, also after they are changed elsewhere. Light and dark mode and every accent colour were checked on every screen and dialog.
- **Clearer help texts** for the Home button, screenshots and recordings.
- **Game list cache:** NEXUS no longer reads the game list cache that the engine's upstream project keeps, and removes it from NEXUS's own engine data. Artwork comes from the game files, your own artwork and the NEXUS artwork database.
- **NEXUS Engine 0.5.0:**
  - upstream Lichii build-160, plus NEXUS patch 0015;
  - shortcuts with modifier keys;
  - the record key;
  - running in the background without input;
  - screenshots on request from NEXUS.

## Bug Fixes

- The *Appearance* page could show *Dark* and the previous accent after the theme had changed.
- A saved recording's length could be reported as 0 seconds.
- The help text for the Home transition still said the game always pauses.
- Folders that are not save data (cache, temporary and system folders) could be offered as saves when importing.

## Known Issues

- **NEXUS is not code-signed.** NEXUS has no code-signing certificate yet. The first time you run it, Windows SmartScreen may show *Windows protected your PC*. The SHA-256 above lets you check that the download is intact.
- **Screen recording was tested on Windows with Microsoft's software encoder and a test window**, in automated builds:
  - the recording, its sound, the microphone track and reading the file back were checked;
  - recording on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel hardware encoders, and recording a real game, were not tested before this release.

  If a recording fails, NEXUS shows the reason. Turning off *Use the graphics card to encode* falls back to software encoding.
- **Game sound on Windows 10.** Recording only one app's sound needs Windows 11. On Windows 10, NEXUS records all sound playing on the PC instead, and *Settings › Screen recording* says so.
- **Running in the background** keeps the game going on the CPU and graphics card. On a slower PC, choose *Pause the game* for the Home button if NEXUS feels slow while a game is in the background.
- Compatibility of individual games depends on the engine and varies by game.
