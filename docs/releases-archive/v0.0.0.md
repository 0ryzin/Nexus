**NEXUS 0.0.0 — the first public preview for Windows (64-bit).**

### Download
Download **`NEXUS-0.0.0-Windows-x64.zip`** below, extract the whole ZIP anywhere, and run `NEXUS\NEXUS.exe`. You don't need to install anything else: the NEXUS Engine and the Microsoft runtime it needs are in the folder. You need your own games, keys and firmware; none are included.

### What's in it
- **A console-style home for your games.** Startup animation, home screen, game library with artwork, search and sorting, controller, keyboard and mouse navigation.
- **NEXUS runs the games itself.** The NEXUS Engine is built into NEXUS, so there is no separate emulator to install or choose. It's based on Lichii build-150 and includes its latest fixes for DLSS, FSR, Vulkan memory, textures and audio.
- **First-time setup.** Choose a name, a picture and your colour. You can have up to 8 users, each with their own saves, play time, theme and controller layout.
- **Frame generation and DLSS** (*Settings › Emulation settings › Frame generation & DLSS*, or per game):
  - **Frame generation:** AMD FSR 3 frame generation (built in), or Lossless Scaling frame generation using your own copy of Lossless Scaling. Choose 2×, 3× or 4× and an optional target frame rate.
  - **NVIDIA DLSS:** used in place of a game's own DLSS on GeForce RTX cards. It needs NVIDIA's Streamline files, and NEXUS shows where they go.
  - **AMD FSR 4:** available on Radeon RX 9000 cards.
  - NEXUS detects your graphics card and only offers what it supports.
- **Controllers.** Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and most USB/Bluetooth pads are detected automatically.
  - Choose Nintendo or Xbox button positions and the controller type the game sees.
  - Move any button to another, for all games or for a single game.
  - Some party games come with a Joy-Con preset.
- **Light and dark themes** throughout, with your profile colour as the highlight. Animated wallpapers (GIF, WebP, MP4, WebM) are supported.
- **Optimise for my device.** NEXUS reads your PC and suggests Performance, Balanced or Quality settings. You see every change before it's applied.
- **Readable save folders** (`Saves\<user>\<game>`), with export and import.
- **Game volume from 0% to 200%**, per game.

### Please note
- **This is a preview.** The engine is built and tested automatically, and NEXUS loads it from the extracted ZIP. Real games haven't been tested by the project yet, though, because the build machines have no graphics card or games. Expect problems, and please report what you find.
- Switch 2 support is experimental.
- NEXUS is not affiliated with Nintendo.
