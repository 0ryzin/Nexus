> **Test build (pre-release).** NEXUS 0.8.0 is the first version with Linux and Steam Deck downloads (0.7.0 was never released). Tested automatically and partly on a Steam Deck by the owner: Ocarina of Time (Switch 2) and Final Fantasy Resonance (Switch 1) start in Gaming Mode. Items marked *not tested on a Deck* below are not confirmed on the hardware yet.

## New Features

- **Steam Deck, 0.8.0 additions** (not tested on a Deck unless stated):
  - *Add NEXUS to Steam*: one button adds NEXUS to the Steam library with its name, icon and artwork (only while Steam is closed; backup kept; other shortcuts untouched).
  - The in-game performance overlay now works on Linux and the Steam Deck (Gaming Mode: drawn through gamescope like Steam's own overlay), with GPU load and memory, temperatures, clocks, power and battery.
  - A back button (L4 by default) returns to NEXUS Home while the game keeps running.
  - Game artwork (cover, background, title logo) follows your NEXUS account between PC and Deck.
  - *Send this device's logs to my other devices*: a Deck's logs reach your PC through your account.

- **Linux and Steam Deck.**
  - One AMD64 download for every AMD64 Linux PC and the Steam Deck, plus ARM64. Each comes as an AppImage (everything inside) or a tar.gz.
  - On the Steam Deck: add the AppImage to Steam in Desktop Mode and start it in Gaming Mode. NEXUS opens full screen, games open on top, and you return to NEXUS when a game closes.
  - On Linux, NEXUS tells you about new versions; download them from the release page.
- **OOT Launch**, a second startup animation (*Settings › General › Startup animation*), with a gold Home background. The standard animation stays the default.
- **Restore defaults** for every setting, *Reset all settings* and *Restore all keybinds*. Each asks first and says what is kept. Users, saves, games, keys, firmware and the GitHub connection are never reset.
- **On-screen keyboard** for every text field when you use a controller.
- **Search in Settings** and in a game's own settings.
- **Game menu with a controller on Home:** X (the Search button) on a game opens the same menu as a right-click.
- **Switch 2 Pro Controller over USB** (experimental, not yet tested with the controller). On Linux it needs a one-time permission rule, explained in README-Linux.txt; NEXUS never changes your system by itself.

## Improvements

- **Frame generation shows what is really happening.** While a game runs, NEXUS shows whether frame generation is running or why it is not. The built-in method is named for what it is: NEXUS's own frame interpolation for any Vulkan graphics card.
- **GitHub stays connected:** NEXUS renews the sign-in by itself. Connect once more after updating.
- **Settings regrouped** into clear sections, with *Changed* marks and *Advanced* parts.
- **Faster start** when the startup animation is off.
- **Switch 1 fixes from Lichii build 341:**
  - a CPU recompiler race;
  - depth problems at higher resolutions;
  - VP9 video;
  - black water in Switch Sports.

## Bug Fixes

- Typing H, X, Q or E in a search or e-mail field no longer goes Home, opens Options or switches tabs.

## Known Issues

- Linux: Switch 2 games' AV1 movies do not play yet.
- Linux: DLSS and FSR 4 are offered on Windows only.
- Linux ARM64: games are less mature than on AMD64. No game has been tested on ARM64.
- Switch 2 controllers over Bluetooth, and Joy-Con 2, are not supported on Linux.
- Nothing in this release has been tested on a Steam Deck yet.

## Future Development

- Steam Deck testing and tuning.
- AV1 movies on Linux.
